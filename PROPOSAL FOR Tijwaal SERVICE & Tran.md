PROPOSAL FOR Tijwaal SERVICE & Transportation PLATFORM 
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY 
•	This proposal outlines the design and development of a Service & Delivery Platform that enables customers to book home and transport services through a bidding-based workflow, supported by wallet-based payments, map-based discovery, and admin-controlled configurations. 
•	The platform will serve as a scalable, secure, and high-performance solution tailored for Saudi Arabia, with architecture designed for future global expansion. 
2. PROJECT OBJECTIVES 
•	Enable customers to request and compare services through competitive bidding. 
•	Empower service providers with flexible job bidding and wallet-based earnings. 
•	Provide administrators full control over services, fees, taxes, and disputes. 
•	Ensure transparency, auditability, and real-time tracking across all workflows. 
3. PLATFORM SCOPE 
•	Applications Included: Customer Mobile Application (iOS & Android), Service Provider App (Mobile + Web Dashboard), Admin Control Panel (Web-based). 
•	Custom Backend & APIs. 
4. SUPPORTED SERVICE CATEGORIES 
•	Home Services: Electrician, Plumber, Carpenter, General maintenance services, etc. 
•	Transport & Delivery Services: Pickup & drop, Item delivery, Flatbed & goods transport. 
5. USER ROLES & ACCESS LEVELS 
•	Customer: Requests services, Compares bids, Makes payments, Tracks jobs and submits reviews. 
•	Service Provider: Bids on service requests, Executes assigned jobs, Manages wallet and withdrawals. 
•	Admin: Approves providers, Configures platform rules, Manages finances, disputes, and analytics. 
6. FUNCTIONAL SCOPE 
•	6.1 Customer Application: Includes phone number login with OTP, profile management, service request with map-based locations, bid comparison, real-time job tracking, wallet payments, and a review/support system. 
•	6.2 Service Provider Application: Includes OTP registration, document upload, live GPS sharing, bidding on requests, job execution with live tracking, wallet balance and withdrawal requests, and customer feedback viewing. 
•	6.3 Admin Panel: Includes user/provider management, service category creation, configuration of fees and taxes, dispute intervention, wallet/financial management, and exportable analytics reports. 
7. PAYMENT & WALLET ARCHITECTURE 
•	Customer payments processed via payment gateway; provider wallet credited after job completion; platform fee and government tax auto-deducted; separate ledger entries for transparency; admin wallet accumulates fees and taxes; secure withdrawals for providers and admin. 
8. MAP, LOCATION & TRACKING 
•	Interactive map-based location selection, radius-based provider filtering, live GPS tracking, and ETA calculation. 
9. NOTIFICATION SYSTEM 
•	Push notifications for job events, SMS for OTP and critical alerts, and Admin-configurable templates.
